Who does landscaping and lawn care in Big Sandy, TX?

Yard Dog Landscapes is a family-owned landscaping and lawn care company out of Longview, and Big Sandy is in our Upshur County service area. Miller Maines founded the company in 2017. The crew is Miller and four others, the same people every visit, and we never sub the work out. We are fully insured and hold a 5.0 rating from 143 Google reviews. Send a quote request any time for a free walkthrough and a written, itemized estimate, usually within a day.

The town takes its name from Big Sandy Creek, and the name fits the ground. The soil around here is mostly sand, deep in places, with clay underneath in the low spots. It drains fast on the high ground and stays wet in the creek bottoms. A lot of homes sit on an acre or several, with pines and hardwoods along the edges and open lawn in between. Lawns burn up quickly in a dry August, and beds need help holding water.

Big Sandy is an easy drive west from our shop on US 80, so it works for weekly mowing as well as bigger installs. Acreage changes the job, so we walk the property before we price it and look at the trim work, the fence lines and where water runs after a rain. If you live between Big Sandy and Hawkins or up toward Gilmer, send your address with a quote request and we will confirm it is in range.

What Big Sandy yards deal with

Sand up high, wet down low

Yards on higher ground drain in hours and dry out fast in summer. Near the creek and in low pockets, clay under the sand keeps water sitting after a heavy rain.

Acreage means trim work

Many homes sit on an acre or more. Fence lines, outbuildings and long drives add trim time, and that is what moves the mowing price more than open turf.

Tree lines and open middles

Pines and hardwoods ring a lot of lots, with open sun in the middle. That is Bermuda in the open and St. Augustine only where the shade is real.

Leaves blow in from the edges

The tree line drops needles and leaves onto the lawn edges and beds every fall, and the wind spreads them across the open yard.

Big Sandy questions

Does Yard Dog work in Big Sandy?

Yes. Big Sandy is out US 80 west of Gladewater, and it is part of our Upshur County service area. We do mowing, beds, sod, drainage, patios, retaining walls and Christmas lights out here. Send your address with a quote request, and we will set up a free walkthrough.

How do you price mowing on acreage in Big Sandy?

A typical residential yard starts at $65 per visit for weekly mowing. On an acre or more, the price depends on how much is open turf versus fence line, outbuildings and trim work. We walk it and give you a flat per-visit price in writing.

Part of my yard stays wet for days after rain. Is that normal here?

It is common in the low spots around Big Sandy, where sand sits on a clay layer. Water drains through the sand, hits the clay and spreads out. A regrade, swale or French drain usually fixes it. Most drainage projects run $2,000 to $3,750, with small fixes starting around $450.

What do Christmas lights cost in Big Sandy?

Most homes start at $900. We supply the lights, custom-cut professional C9 lines to the roofline, install them, take them down after the season and store them. We quote from a photo mock-up, so you can text us a picture and get a number without waiting on a visit.
