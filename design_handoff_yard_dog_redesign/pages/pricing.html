What Changes Your Price

Why two Longview yards get two different numbers.

✓Yard & area size — square footage is the biggest driver on most jobs.
✓Materials you choose — sod, mulch, pavers, plants, or stone set the starting point.
✓Site access — a tight gate or steep spot takes more time than a drive-up lot.
✓Plant selection — mature specimens cost more than starter plants.
✓Wall height & linear footage — taller, longer walls need more base and engineering.
✓Site prep — sandy loam over clay sometimes needs leveling, grading, or drainage first.
