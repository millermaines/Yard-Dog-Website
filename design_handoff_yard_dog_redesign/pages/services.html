East Texas Specifics

What "yard work" actually looks like out here.

Yard Dog Landscapes has been working East Texas yards since 2017, and the services we offer reflect what this specific region actually needs. The Piney Woods climate, sandy-acidic soil, and a long hot growing season mean a yard-maintenance program that works in Dallas or Houston usually doesn't translate here without some tuning.

Every job starts with us walking the property in person and sending a written, itemized estimate. We don't sub the work out, we use the same crew on your visits, and we'll tell you straight if something is going to underperform in this climate before we install it.
