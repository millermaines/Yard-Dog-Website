Gilmer sits in sandy country. Upshur County is known for sweet potatoes, which is what the East Texas Yamboree celebrates every October, and the loose, sandy ground that grows them drains fast and holds little water or fertilizer. Lawns here dry out quickly in a July dry spell, and beds need organic matter worked in to hold moisture between rains. Older lots in town tend to have big shade trees. Outside town, along US 271 and the county roads, homes often sit on acreage with pines along the edges and a lot of open, sunny ground.

What Gilmer yards deal with

Sweet potato sand

Much of the ground around Gilmer is loose sand that drains in hours. Lawns go dry fast in summer, so mowing tall and watering deep and less often matters more here than in heavier soil.

Fertilizer slips through

Sandy soil does not hold nutrients long. Slow-release feedings spread across the season beat one heavy application that washes past the roots.

Rolling ground washes

Upshur County is rolling country, and bare sand on a slope cuts gullies in a hard rain. Groundcover, mulch and a well-placed swale keep the soil where it belongs.

Clay under some low spots

Sand on top does not always mean a yard drains. Where a clay layer sits underneath, low spots still hold water after a long rain, so we check before assuming.

Gilmer questions

Do you service Gilmer and the rest of Upshur County?

Yes. Gilmer is straight up Highway 300 from our shop in Longview, and Upshur County is part of our service area along with Gregg, Harrison, Smith, Rusk, Panola and Nacogdoches counties. If you are out toward Ore City or East Mountain, text us with the address and we will confirm.

How much is lawn mowing in Gilmer?

Weekly mowing for a typical residential yard runs about $50 to $65 a visit. Bigger lots and acreage cost more, and we price those after walking the property, since fence lines, outbuildings and long drives add trim time. You get a flat per-visit price in writing before we start.

Can you fix a sandy lawn that will not stay green?

Often, yes. Sandy lawns brown out fast because water and nutrients drain through quickly. We raise the mowing height, test the soil, and set up slow-release feedings timed for East Texas. If the turf is too thin to come back, new sod over prepped soil with compost worked in is the reset.

Do you do Christmas lights in Gilmer?

Yes, and most homes start at $900. We supply the lights, custom-cut professional C9 lines to your roofline, take them down after the season and store them. Booking opens in early October, the same month as the Yamboree, and the install calendar fills up from there.
