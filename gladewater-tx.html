Gladewater sits where US 80 crosses US 271, mostly in Gregg County with part of town in Upshur County. The older homes around downtown sit under mature pines and hardwoods, with shaded lawns and leaf-filled beds every fall. Outside town the lots open up into acreage with woods, fence lines and long drives, and some properties sit out toward Lake Gladewater. The ground is the same sandy loam over clay found across this part of East Texas.

That mix shapes the work. In town that usually means mowing, hedges, leaf cleanup and bed refreshes under big trees. On the larger properties it's more about keeping a big lawn cut on a steady schedule, trimming along fences and ditches and solving drainage across a whole lot instead of one corner. Gladewater is a little farther out than White Oak, so we'll be straight with you about how often we can be there before you sign up.

What Gladewater yards deal with

Big trees in town

Older Gladewater streets have tall pines and hardwoods over the yards. Expect a heavy leaf drop, shaded St. Augustine and beds that need shade plants and mulch more than sun lovers.

Acreage outside town

Larger lots past the edge of town mean longer mowing days, more fence line to trim and drainage that has to be solved across a whole property.

Sand over clay

Like most of Gregg County, Gladewater ground is sandy on top with clay below. It drains well until the top saturates, and then low spots stay wet after a long rain.

Gladewater questions

Do you serve the Upshur County side of Gladewater?

Yes. We cover all of Gladewater, on both the Gregg and Upshur County sides, plus the rural properties around town. Upshur County is part of our regular service area.

Is Gladewater too far out for weekly mowing?

No. It's an easy drive west on US 80 from Longview. Weekly mowing runs about $50 to $65 per visit for a typical yard, and larger acreage is priced on its own after we walk it.

How much does landscaping cost in Gladewater?

Bed refreshes start at a few hundred dollars, and most landscaping and planting projects run $900 to $3,600. Mulch refreshes run $600 to $2,400, hedge trimming $150 to $800 and drainage projects $2,000 to $3,750. Your exact price comes in writing after a free walkthrough.

Do you work on properties near Lake Gladewater?

Yes, on the land side: lawns, beds, retaining walls, drainage and cleanups. Docks, boathouses and seawalls are not our work.
