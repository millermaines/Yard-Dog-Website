Hardscaping & Patios for Kilgore yards

We built two Kilgore hardscapes this year: a checkerboard concrete paver patio with gravel joints off a covered porch, and a flagstone walkway with chopped stone borders and path lighting. Both sit on a dug-out, compacted base so the sandy ground doesn't shift them. Patio and walkway projects mostly fall between $3,100 and $9,100.

A patio is only as good as what's under it. We dig to the right depth, lay and compact a real base, and pitch the surface so water runs away from the foundation, not toward it. Our clay swells when it's wet and shrinks when it dries, and a patio set on loose fill can look great in May and sink by August.

How the job goes

We build patios year-round in East Texas, and spring and fall book up first. Base work goes best in a dry stretch, because saturated clay won't compact. If you want to be sitting on it by a certain season, call a few weeks ahead.

  1. Walk the site

    We look at the slope, where water runs, how the space connects to the house, and how we'll get material in.

  2. Plan and price it

    You get a written, itemized estimate with the material, size and base spec. If you want to see it first, the $449 Hardscape Preview gives you layout renderings.

  3. Dig and build the base

    We excavate, lay and compact the base, and set the pitch so water sheds away from the house.

  4. Lay the surface

    Pavers or stone go down on the prepared base, cut to fit, with edging or borders to hold the field tight.

  5. Finish and clean up

    We fill the joints, restore the ground around the work, and haul off the spoil.

Hardscaping & Patios questions in Kilgore

What are gravel joints on a paver patio?

Instead of tight joints filled with sand, the pavers are spaced and the gaps are filled with gravel. On the checkerboard patio we built in Kilgore, that gravel gives the pattern its look and lets rain soak through instead of running off toward the house.

How much does a paver patio cost in Longview, TX?

Most patio and hardscape projects run $3,100 to $9,100. A small patio or walkway costs less, and a large outdoor living build with walls, a fire pit or a kitchen costs more. Square footage, the material you pick, and how much grading and base work the site needs drive the number. We walk it for free and send a written, itemized estimate.

What kind of hardscaping do you build?

Paver and flagstone patios, walkways and stepping-stone paths, fire pits and outdoor fireplaces, seating walls, retaining walls, and outdoor kitchens with grill stations. Recent jobs include a flagstone walkway with path lighting and a checkerboard paver patio in Kilgore, and a Cherokee flagstone patio around a chopped stone fire pit in White Oak.

How long does a patio take to build?

Most residential patios and walkways take a few days to a couple of weeks. Size matters, but so does the ground. A site that needs grading or drainage work takes longer than a flat, open backyard, and a long wet stretch can push the dig back. You get a timeline in your written estimate before we start.
