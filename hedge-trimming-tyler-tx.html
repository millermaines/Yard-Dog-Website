Hedge Trimming for Tyler yards

Older Tyler homes often have formal foundation hedges and boxwood borders that need a steady hand, not a fast pass with a power trimmer. We shape from the bottom up, keep the natural form of the plant, and haul off every clipping. We can come once to get things back in shape or set up recurring trims, and most jobs run $150 to $800.

Good hedge trimming is more than running a power trimmer across the top. We shape each hedge a little wider at the base than at the top so light reaches the lower branches, and we keep the natural form of the plant instead of shearing everything into the same box. A hedge that's narrower at the bottom goes bare and leggy underneath.

How the job goes

Most East Texas hedges want two to three trims between spring and fall, when they put on the most growth. June is the clean window for hollies and boxwoods, and right after bloom is the only safe time for azaleas and mophead hydrangeas. Skip hard shearing in late fall, because the tender regrowth burns brown in the first cold snap.

  1. Walk the beds

    We count the shrubs, check their size and condition, and note which ones bloom on old wood so we don't cut off next year's flowers.

  2. Quote it in writing

    You get an itemized price, usually within a day. Most jobs fall between $150 and $800.

  3. Shape by hand

    Tarps go down, then each hedge is shaped to its natural form, a little wider at the base so the lower branches keep their leaves.

  4. Clean up and haul off

    Every clipping is raked up, blown out of the beds and hauled away.

  5. Set the next trim

    Most hedges need two to three trims a season. We can put you on a recurring schedule or come back when you call.

Hedge Trimming questions in Tyler

When should I trim the hollies and boxwoods at my Tyler house?

Early summer, after the spring flush of growth. That gives new growth time to harden off before cold weather. Trimming late in fall pushes out tender shoots that the first hard freeze burns brown, and you end up looking at scorched tips all winter.

How much does hedge trimming cost in Tyler, TX?

Most hedge trimming jobs run $150 to $800. A few foundation shrubs fall at the low end, and long rows, tall hedges or shrubs that haven't been touched in years run higher. Hauling off the clippings is part of the job. We walk the yard and send a written, itemized quote, usually within a day. Send a quote request and we'll get back to you within a day.

How often should hedges be trimmed in East Texas?

Two to three times through the growing season for most hedges, spring into fall. Fast growers like ligustrum and photinia tend toward the higher end of that, and slower boxwoods the lower. We set the schedule by what you have planted and how fast it fills in, and we can put you on a recurring trim so it never gets away from you.

When is the best time to trim hollies and boxwoods?

Early summer, once the spring flush of soft growth has hardened off. June is a good, clean window. Late-season shearing forces tender new growth that the first hard cold snap burns brown, and you stare at scorched tips all winter. If you keep a holly for its red winter berries, trim it lighter, since heavy shearing cuts off part of the crop.
