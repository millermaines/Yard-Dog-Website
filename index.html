How a job actually goes

Four steps, and you'll know the cost and the timing before anyone starts work.

  1. 01 You send photos Three or four pictures of the problem area and a rough idea of what you want. That's usually enough for us to tell you whether we're the right crew for it. Reply the same day, Mon–Sat

Questions we get every week

How much does landscaping cost in Longview, TX?

It depends on the work. Routine lawn maintenance runs about $50 to $65 per visit for a standard residential yard. Drainage projects mostly land $2,000–$3,750, paver patios $3,100–$9,100, and retaining walls $3,000–$19,000 by height and length. We don't quote sight-unseen — we walk the property and put a written, itemised number on it.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call (903) 844-6877 or send the quote form and we'll be in touch within 24 hours to schedule a free on-site walkthrough. You get a clear written estimate with materials separated from labour.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Longview and serve the surrounding East Texas communities: White Oak, Kilgore, Gladewater, Hallsville, Marshall, Tyler, Henderson, Carthage and Nacogdoches. Drive times are listed in Where we work above.
