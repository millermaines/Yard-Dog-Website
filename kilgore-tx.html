What Kilgore yards deal with

Heavy canopy

Pecans, post oaks and pines cover a lot of Kilgore's older streets. That shade thins Bermuda, so most of those lawns are St. Augustine, and it means a big leaf drop every fall.

Sandy, acidic soil

Decades of pine needles push Kilgore soil acidic, and the sand lets water and fertilizer wash through quickly. Soil tests and compost in the beds make a real difference here.

Water on flat ground

Sandy topsoil drains well, but flat lots with clay or a hardpan layer underneath still pond after heavy storms, especially where downspouts empty.

What have you built in Kilgore recently?

Why is my Kilgore lawn thin under the trees?

Usually shade. Bermuda needs full sun, and under Kilgore's pecans and pines it fades out. St. Augustine handles partial shade better, and a higher mowing height helps any lawn hold on. Where the canopy is too dense for any grass, a bed with mulch and shade plants is the better answer.
