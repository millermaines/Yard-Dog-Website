Leaf Removal for Nacogdoches yards

Nacogdoches yards under tall pines and oaks get needles and leaves from October into February, and a wet leaf mat over shaded St. Augustine through winter is how lawns come up dead in spring. Recurring cleanups are hard to run from Longview, but a one-time cleanup paired with bed work or pruning can make sense. Leaf removal is quoted after a walkthrough.

Leaf removal here is not one Saturday with a rake. Pecans drop first, sweetgums let go in early November, and post oaks and water oaks hang on to dead leaves and drop them a few at a time into February. We time cleanups to what is actually falling on your lot, not to a date on the calendar.

How the job goes

Removal starts in the first half of November, when the sweetgums let go and the volume roughly triples in a few days. Plan on another pass in mid-December and a last one before spring green-up, because post oaks and water oaks drop into February. Before November, the light drop is usually better mulched into the lawn with the mower.

  1. Walk the lot

    We look at the trees, the grass under them and how much is already down, then send a written quote, usually within a day.

  2. Plan the passes

    Most lots need a pass in mid-November and another in mid-December. Heavy canopy over St. Augustine can need weekly clearing for three or four weeks.

  3. Clear and collect

    Lawn, beds, walks and drives get cleared, and everything is bagged or hauled off the property.

  4. Clean up the edges

    We blow out rock beds, re-edge bed lines the leaves buried and leave the hard surfaces clean.

  5. Last pass before green-up

    Post oaks and water oaks keep dropping into February, so a late pass uncovers the lawn before spring.

When should leaves be removed in East Texas?

Start collecting in the first half of November, when the sweetgums drop and a mower can't keep up anymore. Plan on another pass in mid-December. Post oaks and water oaks hold dead leaves all winter and let them go into February, so a final cleanup before spring green-up is worth it. The light October drop can just be mulched in.

Can I just mulch my leaves with the mower?

Early in the season, yes. The first light drop in October can go back into the lawn with two passes of the mower while the leaves are dry. The test is simple: if you can still see green grass when you're done, you did it right. If it reads brown, you put a blanket on the lawn and it's time to pick them up.
