Retaining Walls for Tyler yards

Tyler lots with a drop toward the street or a back fence are where walls make the most sense, turning a slope you can't use into level yard. We build segmental block or natural stone on a compacted base set below grade, with gravel backfill, a drain to daylight and geogrid on taller walls. Most walls fall between $3,000 and $19,000, with height and length setting the number.

A retaining wall has one job: hold back dirt that wants to move. Done right, it turns a slope into flat, usable yard, stops erosion and keeps water off the foundation. Done wrong, it leans, bows and cracks within a few seasons. The difference is almost always in the parts you can't see.

How the job goes

We build walls year-round. A dry stretch is the best time to dig and compact, since wet clay won't pack down right. If a slope is already washing out or a wall is starting to lean, don't wait for the spring rains to make it worse.

  1. Read the slope

    We walk the site, measure the grade change, find where the water comes from, and figure out what the wall has to hold.

  2. Spec and price the wall

    You get a written, itemized estimate with the material, height, length and drainage. If the wall is tall enough to need a stamped engineering plan, we tell you at the walkthrough.

  3. Dig and set the base

    We excavate below grade, compact a crushed stone base and set the first course dead level.

  4. Build it up with drainage

    Each course goes up with gravel backfill behind it, a perforated drain at the bottom, and geogrid on taller walls.

  5. Finish and grade

    We finish the top of the wall, grade the ground above and below it so water moves off, and haul away the spoil.

Retaining Walls questions in Tyler

Do taller retaining walls in Tyler need an engineer?

Walls over about four feet usually need engineered design and reinforcement. We handle the base prep, geogrid and drainage those walls need, and if your project calls for a stamped engineering plan, we'll tell you during the walkthrough.

How much does a retaining wall cost in Longview, TX?

Most walls we build run $3,000 to $19,000, and height and length move the number most. A short garden wall starts at a few thousand dollars. A tall structural wall with geogrid and drainage runs $20,000 or more. The material and how much digging the slope needs matter too. We walk the site and send a written, itemized estimate before any dirt moves.

What is the best retaining wall material for East Texas?

For most yards, segmental block from brands like Belgard and Pavestone. It locks together, flexes with the ground and goes up clean. Natural stone and boulder walls cost more but look great on the right property. Pressure-treated timber fits some jobs, like the timber wall we built for a raised boat and RV pad in White Oak.

Do retaining walls need drainage?

Yes, and in East Texas clay it's the most important part of the build. Water that collects behind a wall builds pressure and pushes it out over time. Every wall we build gets gravel backfill and a perforated drain that carries water to daylight or a pop-up emitter, so that pressure never gets a chance to build.
