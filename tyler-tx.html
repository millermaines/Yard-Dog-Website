What Tyler yards deal with

Azaleas with decades on them

Older Tyler neighborhoods, the Azalea District most of all, have azaleas and dogwoods that have been growing for decades. Azaleas get pruned right after the spring bloom, never sheared in fall.

Roses in the beds

If you've got roses in a Tyler bed, give them six or more hours of sun and room for air to move. Knockout roses get cut back hard in February.

Builder beds on newer lots

Newer Tyler subdivisions often come with shrubs planted too close to the foundation and thin mulch over compacted fill. Rebuilding those beds starts with the soil, not the plants.

Slopes that need holding

Where a lot drops toward the street or the back fence, rain washes out beds and undercuts walkways. That's where a retaining wall or a terraced bed earns its cost.
